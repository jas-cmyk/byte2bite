ABOUT US

The story behind the bytes and the bites.

From a small kitchen to your table

Byte2Bite began as a tiny online-only kitchen with one goal: make great Korean comfort food easy to order and fast to reach. What started with a handful of recipes shared between friends is now a neighbourhood favourite.

We cook in small batches, plate every order by hand, and keep the menu honest — no shortcuts, just food we would happily eat ourselves.
